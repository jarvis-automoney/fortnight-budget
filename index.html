Frequently asked questions

The honest answers you'd expect from an Australian budgeting app.

Can I use it without linking my bank?
Yes. Full manual mode works without linking any accounts. Type your transactions in plain English. No bank login required.
Do you support Open Banking?
Yes. We use the Consumer Data Right (CDR) standard — the same system CommBank and other AU banks use. Read-only, bank-grade encryption. We never store your banking credentials.
Is this just another PocketSmith or YNAB clone?
No. Those apps assume monthly budgeting. Fortnight Budget is built for the pay cycle that dominates Australia. The calendar view, bill allocation, and "available until next pay" balance are all designed around your actual pay rhythm — something no major app does well.
Can I import from Pocketbook?
Yes. We'll support CSV import from day one. If you have Pocketbook data, you can bring it with you.
